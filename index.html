How to use

  1. Say who you are. Tap your name. New here? Tap ➕ New name, type your first name, pick a friend and tap Save.
  2. Find a lesson. Tap your grade, open a topic and tap a lesson. You can also type in the 🔎 box.
  3. Learn and play! Tap 🏠 at the bottom of a lesson to come back here.
  4. See your progress. A lesson you opened gets ✓ Opened. A lesson you finished gets ⭐ Done.

Keep your progress safe

  • 📱 Your stars are saved on this device, in this browser. Use the same one next time.
  • 🧹 Clearing the browser history, or a private window, removes your stars.
  • 👫 Sharing a tablet? Tap Not …? and choose your own name before you start.
  • 🧑‍🏫 Teacher on the board? Tap Just looking so nothing is saved.
  • 🔒 Use only your first name. Nothing is sent over the internet.
