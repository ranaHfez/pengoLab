Convince Me!

In the first way, why were 4 ones taken away first?
We had 4 ones, so we could take those 4 right away. We still needed to take 2 more, so we opened a ten into 10 ones and took 2 of them.

Remember

When there are not enough ones to take away, we go to the tens and bring one ten to the ones. We open it into 10 ones. Then we can keep taking away.

Think & Solve

13

How can place-value blocks help you find 66 − 8? Explain.
Show 6 tens and 6 ones. Take away 6 ones. We need 2 more, so open a ten into 10 ones and take away 2. 5 tens and 8 ones are left. 66 − 8 = 58.

14 · Make Sense

Kate has 45 marbles. She gives 3 marbles to her brother. How many marbles does Kate have now?
45 − 3 = 42. Kate has 42 marbles. We had 5 ones, enough to take away 3, so no regrouping.

15 · Higher Order Thinking

Sammie has 9 fewer rings than Emilio. Sammie has 7 more rings than Sara. Emilio has 34 rings.
Sammie: 34 − 9 = 25 rings. Sara: 25 − 7 = 18 rings.
