Find 43 − 24 Two Ways

≈ 8 min

Convince Me!

How are the two ways alike? How are they different?
Alike: Both start with 4 tens and 3 ones, both take away 2 tens and 4 ones, both open one ten into 10 ones, and both get 19.
Different: The first way takes away 3 ones first, then opens a ten and takes 1 more. The second way opens a ten first, then takes away all 4 ones.

Remember

Take away the tens from the tens. Take away the ones from the ones. When there are not enough ones, we bring one ten to the ones place and open it into 10 ones. There are many ways to subtract!

Problem Solving · p.244

Think & Solve

≈ 7 min

14 · Model

Anita has $63. She spends $24 and saves the rest. How much does Anita save?
Show 6 tens and 3 ones. Take away 2 tens. We need to take away 4 ones but have only 3, so open a ten: 3 tens and 13 ones. Take away 4 ones. 3 tens and 9 ones are left. 63 − 24 = 39. Anita saves $39.

15 · Higher Order Thinking

Write a subtraction story about 36 − 17. Explain how to solve the problem.
Example: Huda has 36 stickers. She gives 17 stickers to her friend. How many stickers does she have left?
Show 3 tens and 6 ones. Take away 1 ten. We need 7 ones but have only 6, so open a ten: 1 ten and 16 ones. Take away 7 ones. 1 ten and 9 ones are left. 36 − 17 = 19.

16 · Assessment Practice

Draw place-value blocks to find 70 − 11. Which is the difference?

Talk about it

70 has 0 ones. What do we do first to take away 1 one?
We open a ten! 7 tens becomes 6 tens and 10 ones. Now take away 1 ten and 1 one: 5 tens and 9 ones. 70 − 11 = 59.
