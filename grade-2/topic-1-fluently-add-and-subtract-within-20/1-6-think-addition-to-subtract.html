MINI-LESSON

The Big Idea

To solve a subtraction fact, think of the addition fact hiding inside it. Every three numbers make a fact family.

Say it with me: "4 plus what makes 9? Five! So 9 minus 4 is 5."

4 + 5 = 9
5 + 4 = 9
9 − 4 = 5
9 − 5 = 4
