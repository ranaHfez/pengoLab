🧮 Fact Fun Day!

enVision Math · Grade 2 · Topic 1 · Lesson 8
Practice Addition and Subtraction Facts

⭐ Stars: 0

🎯 Learning goals

Students will:

  • Add and subtract within 20 fluently.
  • Use strategies: doubles, make 10, count on, think addition.
  • Explain how addition and subtraction are related.

🧰 Materials

  • Two-color counters, ten-frames
  • Number lines 0–20
  • Whiteboards & markers
  • This page on a screen or tablets

📖 Vocabulary

Fact family · sum · difference · addend · related facts · doubles

⏱️ 45-minute plan

5 minWarm-up: Balloon Pop (tab 1). Quick mental math to wake up brains.
10 minExplore: Show 8 + 6 on a ten-frame. Ask: “How can we get 14 quickly?” Students share strategies with partners.
10 minTeach: Model the 4 strategies below, then fact families: 6 + 8 = 14, 8 + 6 = 14, 14 − 6 = 8, 14 − 8 = 6.
15 minGame stations: Rotate pairs through games 1–5. Students record 3 facts they found tricky.
5 minExit ticket: Write a fact family for 7, 9, 16 and tell which strategy you used.

💡 Strategies

👯 Doubles: 7 + 7 = 14, so 7 + 8 = 15.

🔟 Make 10: 9 + 6 → 9 + 1 + 5 = 15.

👆 Count on: 5 + 3 → say 5, then 6, 7, 8.

🔁 Think addition: 15 − 9 = ? Think 9 + ? = 15.

🗣️ Questions to ask

  • Which strategy is fastest for 9 + 5? Why?
  • How does 6 + 7 = 13 help you solve 13 − 7?
  • Can you build a fact family with 5, 8, 13?

🌈 Differentiation

Support: ten-frames and counters, facts within 10.
On level: games as written.
Challenge: create your own missing-number puzzles for a partner.
