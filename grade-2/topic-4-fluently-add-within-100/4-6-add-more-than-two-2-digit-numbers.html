Ten Friends Bubbles

★ 0

How to play

Look at the number. Pop the bubble that is its ten friend: the ones add up to 10.

Example: the ten friend of 3 is 7, because 3 + 7 = 10. For 24, look at the ones: 4. A friend like 16 works because 4 + 6 = 10.

Another way · order property

Tap two numbers that are ten friends. They join first!

Convince me!

In the book, the order changed to 24 + 16 + 35 + 17. Why did the order change? Why were 24 and 16 added together?

We can add numbers in any order and the sum stays the same. 24 and 16 go together because 4 ones + 6 ones = 10. They are ten friends, so 24 + 16 = 40. Adding a number like 40 is easy!

Pit Stop Pairs

★ 0

How to play

1. Tap two cars that are ten friends (their ones make 10). 2. Type their sum. 3. Keep joining cars until one car is left. That is the total!

Example: cars 24, 18, 36. The ones 4 and 6 make 10, so tap 24 and 36: 24 + 36 = 60. Then 60 + 18 = 78. Finish line!

Partial Sums Factory

★ 0

How to play

1. Add all the tens. 2. Add all the ones. 3. Add the two partial sums to get the Sum.

Example: 24 + 35 + 16 + 17. Tens: 20 + 30 + 10 + 10 = 70. Ones: 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 = 22. Sum: 70 + 22 = 92.

Start with the tens!

Problem solving · page 160

17. Higher Order Thinking Henry is adding 24, 18 and 36. He makes a ten to add. Which ones digits does Henry add first? Explain.

Henry adds the ones 4 and 6 first, from 24 and 36, because 4 + 6 = 10. So 24 + 36 = 60, and 60 + 18 = 78.
