Objective

Students choose and use a strategy (break apart, open number line or hundred chart) to add two-digit numbers within 100, and explain their work.

Vocabulary

  • break apart
  • open number line
  • hundred chart
  • tens · ones

Materials

  • Student book pp. 109–112 (also in the Book Pages section)
  • Hundred chart, place-value blocks
  • This page on the board (pen tool ✏️)

Assessment

  • Game stars (first-try answers)
  • Convince Me! explanation
  • Independent Practice p. 111–112
