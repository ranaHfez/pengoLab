Sally and Nigel's Blocks

≈ 8 min

Convince Me!

How are these statements alike and different?
Cal has 12 fewer blocks than Mia.
Mia has 12 more blocks than Cal.
Alike: Both tell us Mia has more and Cal has fewer, and the difference is 12.
Different: The first sentence starts with the one who has fewer (Cal). The second starts with the one who has more (Mia).

Remember

Find who has more first.
• If we know the smaller amount and need the bigger one → we add.
• If we know the bigger amount and need the smaller one → we subtract.
• To find how many more or how many fewer → we subtract.
