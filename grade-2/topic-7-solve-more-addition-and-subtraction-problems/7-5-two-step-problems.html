Mia's Birds

Convince Me!

Why do you need two steps to solve the problem above?
We don't know how many birds Mia saw in all. We must find that first (15 + 16 = 31). Then we can find how many flew away (31 − 14 = 17). The answer to step 1 helps us solve step 2.

Remember

1. Read the problem. Find the hidden question.
2. Solve the hidden question (Step 1).
3. Use that answer to solve the problem (Step 2).
4. Check: does the answer make sense?
