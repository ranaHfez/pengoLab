✨ Multiplication Facts: 0, 1, 2, 5, 9, 10 ✨

enVision Math · Grade 3 · Topic 2 · Lesson 5  |  ⭐ Total stars: 0

🎯 Learning Goals

  • Fluently multiply by 0, 1, 2, 5, 9 and 10 using patterns and strategies.
  • Explain why a strategy works (arrays, equal groups, skip counting).
  • Use known facts to solve unknown ones. Time: ~45 minutes.
Vocabulary: factor · product · array · equal groups · skip count · double · Zero Property · Identity Property

🧠 Strategy Cards

×0
Any number × 0 = 0. Zero groups, or groups of zero, means nothing! 7 × 0 = 0
×1
Any number × 1 = itself. One group of 8 is 8. 1 × 8 = 8
×2
Double the number. 2 × 6 → 6 + 6 = 12
×5
Skip count by 5s, or half of ×10. Products end in 0 or 5. 5 × 7 = 35
×10
Put a 0 after the number. 10 × 6 = 60 (six tens)
×9
Do ×10, then take away one group. 9 × 6 = 60 − 6 = 54. Digits of the product add to 9!

🎟️ Exit Ticket

1) 9 × 0 = ___   2) 1 × 12 = ___   3) 5 × 8 = ___   4) 9 × 7 = ___   5) Explain how you can use 10 × 7 to find 9 × 7.

Differentiation: Support – counters and skip-count strips. Extend – write word problems or find 9 × 12 using 10 × 12 − 12.
