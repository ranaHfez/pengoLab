✨ 9 as a Factor ✨

enVision Math · Grade 3 · Topic 2 · Lesson 9

⭐ Stars: 0

📋 Lesson Plan

🎯 Objective

Students use patterns and strategies (10 × n − n, the finger trick, digit sums) to find and explain products with 9 as a factor.

📚 Standards

3.OA.C.7 fluently multiply within 100.
3.OA.D.9 identify arithmetic patterns, including in the multiplication table.

🔤 Vocabulary

factor · product · pattern · digit · multiple · skip count

🧰 Materials

Multiplication chart, counters, whiteboards, tablets or a projector for the games below.

⏱️ Suggested timeline (about 60 min)

  1. Warm-up (5 min): 🚀 Skip-Count Rocket – count by 9s.
  2. Explore (10 min): Circle the 9s row on a multiplication chart. What patterns do you see? Share with a partner.
  3. Teach (10 min): Model the strategies below with think-alouds.
  4. Games 1–4 (25 min): Rotate stations or play whole class on the board.
  5. Close (5 min): Exit ticket + sharing.

💡 Strategies to model

1. Ten minus one group

9 × 6 is one group less than 10 × 6. So 60 − 6 = 54.

2. Finger trick

Hold up 10 fingers. Fold down finger n. Fingers on the left are the tens, on the right the ones.

3. Digit pattern

The tens digit is 1 less than n, and the two digits always add to 9. (5 + 4 = 9)
🗣️ Talk moves: "Why does 10 × n − n work?" · "What do you notice about the digits?" · "Convince your partner."

🎟️ Exit ticket

  1. Find 9 × 7 using 10 × 7 − 7. Show your work.
  2. The product 9 × 4 is 36. What is 3 + 6? Why does this happen?
  3. Write one 9s fact you can now solve fast, and how.

🌈 Differentiation

  • Support: multiplication chart, counters, start with 9 × 2 through 9 × 5.
  • Extend: explain why 9 × n = 10 × n − n, or find 9 × 12 with the same idea.

🚀 Warm-Up: Skip-Count Rocket

Count by 9s! Pick the number that fills the yellow blank. 6 rounds.

🎯 Game 1: Ten Minus One

Use 10 × n, then take away n to find 9 × n. Get 6 right!

🖐️ Game 2: Finger Trick Challenge

To find 9 × n, fold down finger number n. Tap the right finger!

🕵️ Game 3: Digit Detective

The digits of every 9s product add up to 9. Find the missing digit!

🃏 Game 4: Memory Match

Flip two cards. Match each 9s fact with its product.
