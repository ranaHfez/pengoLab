🔢 2 and 5 as Factors

enVision Math • Grade 3 • Topic 2, Lesson 1 — Interactive Lesson Plan

📋 Lesson Plan Overview

Standard: 3.OA.A.13.OA.C.7⏱ 45–50 min

Objective: Students will multiply using 2 and 5 as factors by applying skip counting and doubling strategies, and will represent multiplication facts as equal-group arrays.

Vocabulary

  • Factor — a number multiplied to find a product
  • Product — the answer to a multiplication problem
  • Array — objects in equal rows and columns
  • Skip counting — counting forward by a number other than 1

Materials

  • This interactive webpage (device per student/pair)
  • Whiteboard for anchor chart
  • Counters or connecting cubes (optional)
  • Exit ticket (paper or digital)

🗓️ Lesson Flow

  • 1. Warm-Up (5 min): Skip Counting Sprint — activate prior counting-by-5s and counting-by-2s skills.
  • 2. Mini-Lesson (8 min): Teacher models doubling for ×2 and skip counting for ×5 using an array on the board (e.g., 3 rows of 5 counters).
  • 3. Game 1 — Double Trouble (7 min): Practice ×2 facts through doubling.
  • 4. Game 2 — High Five (7 min): Practice ×5 facts through skip counting.
  • 5. Game 3 — Array Builder (8 min): Build arrays to connect facts to visual models.
  • 6. Game 4 — Boss Challenge (7 min): Timed mixed-fact fluency check.
  • 7. Wrap-Up (3 min): Exit ticket — "How does doubling help with ×2? How does skip counting help with ×5?"

🎯 Teaching Tips

  • Emphasize: ×2 = double the number (2 × 6 is the same as 6 + 6).
  • Emphasize: ×5 facts always end in 0 or 5 — great pattern for students to notice.
  • Let students play in pairs and talk through strategies out loud.
  • Use the tabs above to move between activities — no downloads needed.
