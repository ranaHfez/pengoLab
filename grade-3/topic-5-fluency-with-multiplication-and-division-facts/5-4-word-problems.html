Gina has 45 hats. She puts 9 hats in each box. How many boxes will she fill?

45 hats0 boxes
9 hats in each box? boxes
One way: divide
45 ÷ ? = 9
45 ÷ 5 = 9
There are 5 groups of 9 in 45.
Another way: related fact
9 × ? = 45
9 × 5 = 45
So, 45 ÷ 9 = 5.

Gina fills 5 boxes with 9 hats each. 🎩

Convince Me!

Krys has 42 hats. She puts 5 hats in each box. Can she use the same strategies to find how many boxes she needs?

Count by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45. We skip over 42! No 5s fact makes 42, so the hats do not fit in full boxes of 5. Krys fills 8 boxes and has 2 hats left.
