Warm-up: Fact Splash

8 quick facts we already know. Tap the answer!

Strategies we already have

  • Skip count: 5, 10, 15, 20 …
  • Draw a bar diagram or an array
  • Break apart a factor: 6 × 4 = (2 × 4) + (4 × 4)
  • Use a fact I know: 5 × 4 = 20, so 6 × 4 = 20 + 4
  • Turn it around: 3 × 8 = 8 × 3

After each answer, ask: Which strategy did you use?

Learn: One fact, many ways

Pick any fact, then try every strategy.

Solve & Share · page 177

Alfredo has 6 bags of oranges. Each bag has 7 oranges. How many oranges does he have?

Show 2 ways to find the answer.

Way 1 · Skip count by 7s: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42
Way 2 · Use a fact I know: 5 × 7 = 35, then add one more bag of 7 → 35 + 7 = 42
So, 6 × 7 = 42 oranges
