Mission 6 & 7: Space Math Academy

enVision Math Grade 3 · Volume 1 · Topic 3 · L3-3 · Apply Properties: 6 and 7 as Factors

🎯 Mission Goals

  • Use the Distributive Property to break apart 6 and 7 into facts I already know.
  • Find 6 × n as (5 × n) + (1 × n) and 7 × n as (5 × n) + (2 × n).
  • Explain my strategy and solve word problems with 6s and 7s.

📚 Vocabulary

  • Factor: a number you multiply.
  • Product: the answer to multiplication.
  • Distributive Property: break a factor into parts, multiply each, then add.
  • Array: objects in equal rows and columns.

🧰 Materials

  • Counters or grid paper, two colors
  • Mini whiteboards or this page's whiteboard
  • Projector for the games
  • Exit ticket paper

💡 Teacher Tips

  • Say it out loud: "6 is 5 and 1 more."
  • Alternative: 6 × n = double 3 × n.
  • Ask: "Which facts do you already know?" (5s, 1s, 2s)
  • Let students come up and draw the split on the whiteboard.

⏱️ Flight Plan (45 min)

5 min🔥 Warm-Up Launch: fast facts of 1s, 2s, 5s, 10s (Warm-Up tab).
10 min🔭 Explore: build a 6 × 4 array with two colors. What do you see? Discuss.
10 min🧠 Teach: use the Array Lab and Whiteboard to model 6 × 8 and 7 × 6.
15 min🎮 Practice: students pick Games 1 to 4 (pairs or teams).
5 min🎟️ Exit Ticket: solve 6 × 9 and 7 × 7 using a split. Write one sentence on how.

🗣️ Key Questions

  • How can 5 × 8 help you find 6 × 8?
  • Why does 7 split into 5 + 2?
  • Can you find 6 × 7 a different way? (Try 3 × 7 doubled.)
