Learn: How Do You Measure Area?

Cover the shape · No gaps · No overlaps · Count

New words
Area
the number of unit squares needed to cover a region with no gaps or overlaps.
Unit square
a square with sides that are each 1 unit long.
Square unit
the unit we use for area. One unit square has an area of 1 square unit.

Estimate

This oval does not fill whole squares. Sometimes we estimate the area: count the full squares, then put partly filled squares together to make about full squares.

Another Example

Unit squares leave gaps on this octagon. Emily breaks a square into 2 same-size triangles. 2 triangles = 1 square unit.

14 triangles cover the octagon. 14 triangles make 7 squares, so the area is 7 square units.

Convince Me!

Karen says these two shapes each have an area of 12 square units. Do you agree?

The purple shape covers 12 whole squares, so its area is exactly 12 square units. The pink oval has partly filled squares, so 12 square units is only an estimate. Karen is right for the purple shape, and her answer for the oval is a good estimate.
