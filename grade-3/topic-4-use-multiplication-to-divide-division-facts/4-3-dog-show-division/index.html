Learn: Think Multiplication, Write Division

To divide, ask yourself: what number times 6 (or 7) gives me the total?

Step 1 · The Dog Show: 48 dogs, 6 in each group

The judge wants 6 dogs in each group. How many groups will there be? Find 48 ÷ 6.

Step 2 · One more dog arrives! 49 dogs, 7 in each group

Now there are 49 dogs and 7 dogs in each group. Find 49 ÷ 7.

Step 3 · Draw a bar diagram: 36, 6, and 6

The whole bar is 36. Each part is 6. Add parts until the bar is full. How many parts?

36
Parts: 0 · Total so far: 0
What you think
6 × 6 = 36
What you write
36 ÷ 6 = 6
