Use Multiplication to Divide with 8 and 9

I can divide by 8 and 9 by thinking about how I multiply with those numbers.

Warm-up: Legs and Petals

Every spider has 8 legs. Every flower has 9 petals. How many in all?

What multiplication fact can you use?

To divide by 8 or 9, ask: what number times 8 (or 9) makes the total?

John has 56 straws. He needs 8 straws to make a spider. How many spiders can John make?

Straws left: 56

Spiders made: 0

7 × 8 = 56  so  56 ÷ 8 = 7

John can make 7 spiders.

Find 56 ÷ 8. Think: What number times 8 is 56? Tap a number to try it.
