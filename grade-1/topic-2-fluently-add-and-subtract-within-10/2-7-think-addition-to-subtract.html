Think Addition to Subtract!

enVision Math • Grade 1 • Volume 1 • Topic 2 • Lesson 2-7

📋 Full Lesson Plan (45 min)

Big idea: We can use an addition fact we know to solve a subtraction problem. 9 − 4 = ? Think: 4 + ? = 9. 4 + 5 = 9, so 9 − 4 = 5!

🎯 Learning Goals

  • Use addition to solve subtraction within 10.
  • Find a missing addend (4 + ? = 9).
  • Explain: “Addition and subtraction are related.”

🔤 Vocabulary

add, subtract, missing number, related facts, total (whole), part

🧰 Materials

Counters or cubes, ten frames, whiteboards and markers, this web page.

⏱️ Timeline

TimeActivity
5 minWarm-Up game 🎈 – Balloon Pop: find the number that makes the total.
10 minExplore & Teach – Show 9 cubes. Hide 4 under a cup. Ask: “What do we need to add to 4 to get 9?” Reveal 5. Write 9 − 4 = 5 and 4 + 5 = 9 together.
8 minGame 1 🐸 – Think It Through (missing number → answer).
8 minGame 2 🦖 – Fact Detective (pick the helper addition fact).
8 minGame 3 🐙 – Octopus Feast (solve with a number pad).
6 minShare & Exit Ticket – “How can 7 − 3 be solved with addition?” Draw and tell a partner.

🗣️ Teacher Talk

  • “When I see −, I think +!”
  • Ask: “Which number is the whole? Which part do we know?”
  • Use a ten frame: fill to the whole, count the empty spots.

🌟 Differentiation

Support: use counters and totals to 6. Challenge: make up your own pair of facts (like 8 − 3 and 3 + 5).

✅ Check for Understanding

Child can say the matching addition fact and gets the correct difference for 3 of 4 problems.
