Count On to Add

enVision Math · Grade 1 · Topic 2 · Lesson 1

6+3=9
Lesson planWarm-upFrog hopBalloon popCount-on dice

📘 Lesson plan

🎯 Learning goal

Students count on 1, 2, or 3 from the greater number to find a sum.

🧰 Materials

  • Number line 0–20
  • Counters
  • Number cubes
  • This webpage

🗣️ Vocabulary

  • add
  • count on
  • sum
  • plus (+)
5 min1. Warm-up: Number Path

Play the warm-up game below. Ask: "What number comes after 7? After 8?" Students say the numbers aloud.

10 min2. Explore: Counting on with a story

Story: "There are 6 ducks in the pond. 3 more ducks swim in. How many ducks now?"

  • Show 6 counters. Say the big number, 6, without counting them again.
  • Add 3 counters one at a time: "7, 8, 9."
  • Write 6 + 3 = 9. Repeat with 8 + 2 and 5 + 1.
10 min3. Learn: Count on from the greater number

Model the number line. Start on the bigger number and hop the smaller one. Try 2 + 7: start at 7, hop on 2. Ask, "Why start with the bigger number?" (fewer hops).

Say it: "Put the big number in your head. Count on 1, 2, 3."

15 min4. Practice: Games

Students play Frog Hop, Balloon Pop, and Count-On Dice in pairs or on tablets. Walk around and ask, "Where did you start? How many did you count on?"

5 min5. Close: Exit ticket

Ask each student to solve 4 + 3, 9 + 1, and 5 + 2 and tell you where they started.

7, 10, 7. Look for students who start on the greater number.

⚠️ Watch for

  • Counting the first number again (counting "6" as the first hop).
  • Hopping too many or too few times. Have students tap a finger for each count.
