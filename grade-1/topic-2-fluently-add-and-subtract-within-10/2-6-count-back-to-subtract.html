🐸 Count Back to Subtract 🚀

enVision Math · Grade 1 · Volume 1 · Topic 2: Fluently Add and Subtract Within 10 · Lesson 2-6

📋 Lesson Plan

🎯 Objective

Students use counting back to subtract 1, 2 or 3 from numbers within 10, using number lines, counters and equations.

Time: about 45 minutes

🔤 Vocabulary

count backsubtractminus (−)equals (=)differencenumber line

🧰 Materials

Number line 0–10, counters, mini whiteboards, a frog toy or picture, this page on the board.

💬 Key Question

How can counting back help me find 8 − 3?

⏱️ Steps

TimePartWhat to do
5 minWarm-UpPlay “Count Back Warm-Up”. Count backward aloud together from 10 to 0.
10 minExplore (Whiteboard)Pose: “Frog is on 8 and hops back 3. Where does it land?” Students act it out on the whiteboard number line, then with counters.
10 minTeachModel 8 − 3: start at 8 and say “7, 6, 5” while holding up 3 fingers. The last number said is the answer. Write 8 − 3 = 5. Stress: do not count the starting number.
5 minGuided PracticeStudents solve 6 − 1, 9 − 2, 7 − 3 on mini whiteboards and show answers together.
12 minGamesGames 1–4 in pairs, groups or whole class.
3 minExit TicketSolve 10 − 2 and tell how you counted back.

🌱 Support

Use a physical number line and move a finger back. Start with “1 less”.

🚀 Challenge

Ask “9 − ? = 6”, and ask why counting up might be easier for bigger jumps.

✅ Check for Understanding

Can the student start at the right number, hop back the correct number of times, and say the answer?

🔥 Warm-Up: Count Back Warm-Up

Look at the counting-back pattern. What number comes next?

🖍️ Whiteboard

🐸 Game 1: Frog Hop

Press the frog to hop back, then choose where it lands!

🤖 Game 2: Silly Robot Checker

Silly Robot counted back, but he sometimes makes mistakes! Is he right or wrong?

👾 Game 3: Monster Munch

Feed the hungry monster cookies, count back, and tell how many are left!

🎈 Game 4: Balloon Pop

Pop the balloon with the correct answer!
