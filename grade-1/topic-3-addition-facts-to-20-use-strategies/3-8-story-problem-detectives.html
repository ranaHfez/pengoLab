“Fewer than” can mean add!

Tonya reads 5 books. She reads 7 fewer books than Seth. How many books did Seth read?

Read

Tonya reads 5 books. She reads 7 fewer books than Seth.

What do you know?

Tonya read 5 books.
7 fewer than Seth means Seth read 7 more than Tonya.

What do you need to find?

How many books Seth read.

Write an equation

5 + 7 = 12
Seth read 12 books!

Three kinds of story problems

Add more
Ali has 10 marbles. He gets some more. Now he has 16. How many did he get?
Parts make a whole
13 fish swim. 6 are orange. The rest are blue. How many are blue?
Compare
Mia has 8 pens. She has 5 fewer pens than Sam. How many pens does Sam have?

Detective steps

  1. Read
    Read the story two times.
  2. Know
    What numbers do you know?
  3. Find
    What is the question asking?
  4. Show
    Use counters, a drawing, or an equation.
  5. Check
    Does your answer make sense?
