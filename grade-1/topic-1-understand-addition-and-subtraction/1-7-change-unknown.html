🎈 Change Unknown

enVision Math · Grade 1 · Topic 1 · Lesson 7

Lesson Plan 45–50 min

🎯 Objective

Students solve add-to problems where the change (what is added) is unknown, e.g. 4 + ? = 7, using objects, ten-frames and equations.

📚 Standards

CCSS 1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8 (adapt to your district's list)

🔤 Vocabulary

change unknown, start, change, total, equation, missing number

🧰 Materials

Counters or cubes, small cups, ten-frames, whiteboards, a device per pair (for the games)

Lesson Flow (tick as you go)

🌱 Support

  • Keep totals to 5
  • Use real counters under a cup
  • Color the start red, the change yellow

🚀 Extend

  • Totals up to 10 only, no pictures
  • Students write their own story for 3 + ? = 8
  • Find two more equations in the same fact family

💬 Key Questions

  • What do you know? What don't you know?
  • Is the total bigger than the start?
  • How can you check your answer?
